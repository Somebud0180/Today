# Today
### A multimedia journal app
Journaling but easier and richer.

### Features
- **Journal with your words.** Capture the moment, in your words and yourself.
- **Or capture less.** Feel free to shield your device's camera, or capture an audio only entry.
- **Only for you.** Your entries are only seen by you. Secure them safely on device or sync with iCloud as you please.
- **Make it feel you.** With various background, handmade or captured by myself.
- **Keep track.** View your entries day-by-day in the Jog Book. A calendar of your entries.
- **Accessible.** The app supports VoiceOver. Along with other considerations to make the app work better for everyone.
- **Searchable.** Find the day you want. Search by title, day or what you said.
- **Transcribe on device.** Powered by [FluidAudio](https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio). Get a transcript of your entries. For reading or for searching.
